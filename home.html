Our Mission

We connect the top leaders of today and tomorrow in a dynamic forum of exchange.

The Harvard College Project for Asian and International Relations (HPAIR) was founded in 1991 to create a forum of exchange for students and young professionals to discuss and learn about the most important economic, political, and social issues facing the Asia-Pacific region. Since 1991, HPAIR has organized 49 conferences in 34 different host countries, touching the lives of more than 40,000 students and young professionals. A student-run organization at Harvard College

Getting started

Three steps to an account.

Access is limited to HPAIR delegate alumni, so everyone in the directory has attended a conference.

Check your email

Registration is limited to addresses on the HPAIR alumni allowlist. Use the same address you registered for the conference with.

Create your account

Pick a password and the account is active immediately, with no approval step. If you forget it, there's a reset link on the sign-in screen.

Complete your profile

Add a headshot, your class year, what you're working on and where you are. The more you fill in, the easier you are to find in the directory.

Questions

Good to know.

Who can join the alumni portal?
Any past HPAIR delegate whose email address is on the alumni allowlist. If you attended a conference and your address isn't recognized, email tech-help@hpair.org and the team will add you.
I've forgotten my password.
On the sign-in screen, choose Forgot password? and enter your email. You'll get a secure reset link from Firebase. Check your spam folder if it doesn't arrive within a few minutes.
Who can see my profile?
Only signed-in alumni. Your name, headshot, class year, role, location, bio and industries appear in the directory so other delegates can find you. Everything is optional. You choose what to fill in, and you can edit or clear it at any time from Edit my profile.
Can I share a job or an opportunity?
Yes. Every alum can post to the Career Hub. Add a title, organization, description, an application link and an optional closing date, and it is visible to everyone signed in to the portal straight away. You can edit or delete anything you posted.
What does the Ambassador Program involve?
Ambassadors represent HPAIR in their region, mentor incoming delegates, and help shape the next conference. It's a one-year volunteer role. When a round is open you'll find the application form in the Ambassador tab of the portal.
Something's broken. Who do I tell?
Email tech-help@hpair.org with a short description and, if you can, a screenshot. It goes straight to the team that maintains this portal.
